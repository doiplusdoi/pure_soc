# Germany Country Pack

Status: demo/review-required country-pack metadata.

Current source-backed differences captured in code:

- NIS-2 implementation requirements are source-mapped to an effective date of 2025-12-06.
- Covered companies and public authorities use the BSI Portal for NIS-2 registration and security-incident reporting.
- BSI states that NIS-2 registration is not carried out through MIP2.
- KRITIS operators and federal authorities continue to use MIP2 as their primary route during transition workflows.
- European Commission metadata identifies BSI as single point of contact, competent authority, and national CSIRT, but that page is stale and not sufficient for current operational rules.
- German important versus especially important thresholds are not active logic in PureSOC yet.
