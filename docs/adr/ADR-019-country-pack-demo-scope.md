# ADR-019: Country Pack Demo Scope

Status: draft.

## Decision

The partner demo will keep the EU baseline and country-specific logic in country-pack packages. Romania remains backed by existing generated workbook data. Poland and Germany will be demo/unreviewed packs with official source metadata and conservative guidance only.

## Consequences

- No country pack is legally active unless the existing regulatory source activation workflow approves it.
- UI routes must not hardcode country-specific legal rules.
- National wording remains readiness guidance, not legal advice or certification.
